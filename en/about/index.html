Author

Per Högberg

Author of the PHU series. Christian Democrat and Chairman of the Municipal Executive Board of Mullsjö Municipality, Sweden, 2023–2026, with a long background in IT-supported business development and the SAP business system.

Background

Per Högberg is a Christian Democrat and was Chairman of the Municipal Executive Board of Mullsjö Municipality 2023–2026. Before that, he worked in the IT sector 2007–2023 on business development within IT-supported operations in various parts of the world.

He has worked with the SAP business system since 1998. Since 2007 he has taken part in SUGEN (SAP User Group Executive Network), an international network of independent user associations, whose founding he also led.

Since 2006 he has been an industry researcher at Jönköping University, School of Engineering, with information modelling as his main field. With agentic AI, his focus has shifted to how integrated data sharing can change public services and work processes.

He is a member of the Advisory Board of the School of Health and Welfare at Jönköping University.

Roles and interests

  • Chairman of the Municipal Executive Board of Mullsjö Municipality 2023–2026, for the Christian Democrats (KD).
  • Takes part in the Swedish Association of Local Authorities and Regions’ (SKR) committee on workforce supply. The investigations are not commissioned by SKR and do not express SKR’s position.
  • Board member of SAPSA (the Swedish SAP user association), which he represents in SUGEN.
  • Member of the Advisory Board of the School of Health and Welfare at Jönköping University.
  • Owner of C-Business (Collaborative Business Sweden AB), which funds the work on the investigations.
